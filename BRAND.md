# Accord — Brand Model

> The authority on _how Accord presents itself to the world_. Derived from a
> brand-building grilling session (2026-08-04). Decisions below are locked;
> changes require a new grilling round.

## Central Idea

**Justice is infrastructure.** Dispute resolution is a composable primitive —
any program plugs in two CPI calls and gets Schelling-point adjudication.

Credibility layer: _honesty is the profitable strategy._ The mechanism works
because the Schelling Point for subjective questions is truth.

## Brand Lockup

```
ACCORD
Mechanize the verdict.
```

- **Name:** Accord (agreement, harmony — what the Schelling Point produces)
- **Tagline:** Mechanize the verdict.
- **Etymology:** from Latin _ad cordem_ (to heart → agreement). The Schelling
  Point produces an accord: independent agents converge without communication.

## Antagonist

**The forced choice.** Every Solana builder today faces a trap: trust a small
group of humans (centralization, collusion, throughput ceiling) OR ship without
dispute resolution (incomplete protocol, user risk). Accord dissolves the trap —
resolution with far fewer trusted humans (an honest stake majority replaces the
committee; see the [Trust Profile](apps/docs/docs/security/trust-profile.md)
for the full residual set).

Concrete instantiation: UMA whale capture (March 2025) — one actor with 25% of
tokens flipped a $7M Polymarket contract. Token-weighted voting = plutocracy.
Accord's secret, randomly-drawn, slashed jurors make capture structurally
impossible.

## Co-branding

**MetaDAO** — the respected sibling. Same conviction family (mechanism replaces
committee), same community (mtnDAO). Different question-shape:

> MetaDAO answers "what should we do?"
> Accord answers "who's right?"
> A DAO needs both — one to decide, one to settle.

**Kleros** — the proof of concept. 1,000+ disputes on Ethereum since 2019.
Cited as credibility. "The Schelling-point court works. Solana has had nothing
equivalent." Never positioned as competitor or clone-source.

## Voice

Punchy, no-BS, crypto-native. Action verbs, no hedging. The Mechanist speaks in
imperatives. No "we're excited to," no "revolutionary," no "imagine a world
where." Lead with the mechanism, not the vision. Vision is the punchline.
